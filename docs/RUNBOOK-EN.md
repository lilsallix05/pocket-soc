# Pocket SOC — first response

Owner: __________  Technical lead: __________  DPO/contact: __________

1. **Recognise.** Record occurrence and awareness times with timezone. Preserve evidence. Confirm whether personal data is affected.
2. **Contain.** Isolate appropriate systems, restrict compromised access, preserve original logs and record who did what.
3. **Assess.** Record harm, sensitive data, potential misuse/identity fraud and affected people. Separate Commissioner and individual decisions. Explain the planning-clock basis.
4. **Prepare.** Draft facts, consequences, chronology, actions, protective advice and contact details. Have the designated reviewer approve the current official form and record its confirmation receipt.
5. **Follow up.** Track applicable individual and phased-information checkpoints. Record recovery, lessons, owners and evidence retention decisions.

Use the [JPDP DBN Guideline](https://www.pdp.gov.my/ppdpv1/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/GP_DBN_ENG.pdf) and the decision record for the detailed mapping. This working aid is not a legal determination. During practice, send no real notices. Reviewed 19 October 2026.
